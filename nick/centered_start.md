# Centered Start

## Nick Description

This is a start of the day meditation that grounds me with 3 affirmations: thought non-identification, acceptance / welcoming from others, gratitude

## Title

Centered Start

## Description

Centered Start is a short morning meditation with three affirmations and pauses for reflection. It guides you to observe your thoughts, open to connection, and step into the day with calm, clarity, and grounded confidence.

## Meditation Script

```
[Tibetan Singing Bowl]

I am the awareness behind my thoughts and emotions. They arise, they pass, and I remain.

<break time="90s"/>

[Tibetan Singing Bowl]

I step into the center of the circle of 20 people, calm and open. I look around, meet a gaze, and we share a smile. I receive acceptance, and I offer it freely.

<break time="90s"/>

[Tibetan Singing Bowl]

I am grateful for the strength, resources, and capacity within me to meet today's challenges with clarity and energy.

<break time="90s"/>

[Double Tibetan Singing Bowl]
```
