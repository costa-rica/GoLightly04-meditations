# metta_short.md

## Nick Description

This is a happiness meditation. When you wish well on others you feel happy.

## Title

Metta (Love and Kindness)

## Description

Metta meditation often cultivates genuine joy, warmth, gratitude, and emotional well-being. By repeatedly generating loving-kindness, many meditators experience increased happiness, inner calm, connection, and reduced negative emotions over time.

## Meditation Script

[Tibetan Singing Bowl]
<break time="4s" />

For the next minute and a half, silently offer these phrases to yourself.
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May I be safe.{/speed}
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May I be happy.{/speed}
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May I be healthy.{/speed}
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May I live with ease.{/speed}
<break time="90s" />

[Tibetan Singing Bowl]
<break time="3s" />

For the next minute and a half, offer the same kindness outward to someone who naturally evokes warmth.
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May you be safe.{/speed}
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May you be happy.{/speed}
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May you be healthy.{/speed}
<break time="2s" />

{speed=0.9}May you live with ease.{/speed}
<break time="90s" />

[Tibetan Singing Bowl]
<break time="3s" />
